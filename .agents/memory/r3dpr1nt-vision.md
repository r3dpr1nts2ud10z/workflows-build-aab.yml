# R3DPR1NT — the encore product (user's vision, agreed July 12, 2026)

Digital drive-in theater platform; N3PSQAL3 is "the prequel."

## Sequencing decision
**Build AFTER N3PSQAL3's launch settles — do not start alongside it.** User explicitly chose to let N3PSQAL3 launch clean first.
**Why:** protects launch focus; N3PSQAL3 week-2 roadmap (results-verification AI) may also compete for time.

## Foundation choice: "the lot" first
User chose community hub as day-one core — filmmakers, comedians, e-bikers, streamers gather first; theaters/screenings added later.
**Why (user's words):** the crowd becomes "bait" for sponsored features and guest cameos — rising or fading talent connects and expands their brands to audiences seeking "4-D ambience" entertainment. Venue-before-crowd is backwards.

## Key concepts
- Tier gate: N3PSQAL3 subscription tiers double as R3DPR1NT access passes (one subscription, two worlds).
- "Blank screen + signature glasses": prime-time features visible only to paid customers with the signature glasses — exclusivity layer. (Digital analog TBD: e.g., token-gated stream unlock.)
- Content: creator-submitted originals only (premieres, comedy sets, streams). Licensed third-party films flagged as a legal minefield — avoid unless real licensing exists.
- "4-D rollout of a R3DPR1NT series" — a first-party 3-D animation/anime series is part of the vision.

## Later-stage concepts (July 12, 2026 riff)
- "Death Star engine": persistent 3-D city world (user has prior experience with a Cesium-built 3-D NYC) as civilization-simulator endgame that doubles as a weekly episodic series — world-as-venue, episodes-as-events.
- Dead-catalog licensing strategy: acquire cheap legitimate licenses for dormant/forgotten films ("dead tickets") to give them a permanent home — real contracts, sound playbook.
- Bright line agreed: recognizable third-party characters (e.g., Paramount/Fox IP) stay out of anything monetized; flagship series uses original characters the user owns.
